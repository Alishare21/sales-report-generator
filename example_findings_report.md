# Business findings report
Use this as a structural and tone reference only. The reviewable fictional example
is in `outputs/example_retail/findings_report.pdf` and `.xlsx`. Never reuse its data.

## Executive summary
At most five short lines: analyzed scope, main metric, and the leading findings.

## Data overview and methodology
Explain retained rows, removed rows, actual columns used, observed-value coverage,
metric definitions and material assumptions. Name estimated measures explicitly.

## Key findings
Present one evidence-based chart and two or three sentences per finding: the actual
metric, a clear comparison, and the operational implication. Cite its figure.

## Recommendations
Table with priority, specific action and affected group, linked finding, and impact
calculation. Separate review coverage from uncertain incremental business effects.

## Limitations and next steps
Always state missing fields, assumptions, data quality concerns and conclusions the
dataset cannot support. Give the next practical validation or controlled test.

## Appendix
Include the complete cleaning log, metric definitions and column dictionary.
